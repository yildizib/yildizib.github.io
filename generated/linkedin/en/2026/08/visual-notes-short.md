Technical prose shines when its visual aids sit right beside the text they clarify.  

An inline expression like $E = mc^2$ lives inside a sentence, while a display equation such as $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}$ or the quadratic formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ can be presented on its own line.  

Mermaid diagrams let us embed flowcharts, sequence diagrams, state machines, class relationships, pie charts, and even a full e‑commerce order flow directly in the Markdown source.  

Because the explanation, its source, and its visual form share the same file, any update to the narrative automatically stays in step with the accompanying graphics. This tight coupling keeps documentation concise, reproducible, and ready to evolve.
