Visual notes treat diagrams and equations as **executable explanations**, keeping the visual element next to the sentence it clarifies.

When a visual sits beside the text that needs it, the result is strongest, and the idea does not drift into a separate document that requires extra maintenance.

The source catalogues several Markdown‑friendly forms: flowcharts for publishing pipelines, inline $E=mc^2$ and block equations, sequence and state diagrams, small matrices, class diagrams, integrals, pie‑chart distributions, and even a full e‑commerce order flow.

Embedding these visuals directly in the Markdown lets the narrative, its sources, and the visual representation evolve together.
