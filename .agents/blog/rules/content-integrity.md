# Content integrity

Treat the supplied source Markdown as the sole factual authority. Do not invent facts, dates, statistics, citations, quotations, personal experiences, or conclusions that are absent from it. Preserve the source's uncertainty and technical precision. If the source is too thin for a requested claim, omit the claim rather than filling the gap.

Do not present a derivative as an independent original work. It must retain a machine-readable reference to its source file and source commit.
